## cue 01
The formula editing screen. This is where you apply the update suggestions from the previous chapter to the actual search strategy. There are two ways to do it: rewrite a block yourself via the pencil icon, or have the AI redesign the whole block for you.

## cue 02
Let's start by letting the AI do it. Press "Have AI improve this" on block 2. A field opens for your instructions — you can leave it blank, or steer it with something like "add more synonyms." Press "Get suggestion" and it redesigns just that block.

## cue 03
Here's the suggestion. At the top is the reasoning behind the change — in this case, that a MeSH term was added so variant wordings in the text don't slip through. Below that is a before-and-after comparison, so you can see exactly what changes before accepting it.

## cue 04
If you're satisfied, press "Replace with this suggestion"; if not, press "Discard." Accepting swaps the block's expression right away. That's the MeSH term from the previous chapter now in place. From the pencil icon you can fine-tune the result by hand.

## cue 05
Once you've finished your edits, note the reason for the change in the edit memo. Then press "Save as new version," and a new version is recorded with the original as its parent. Open the history and you'll see the one you just saved. The previous strategy isn't lost — you can trace it here later.

## cue 06
This is the result of re-validating with the term added. The paper that was being missed is now retrieved, and the capture rate has reached 100 percent. Building a search strategy is this loop: validate, fix, validate again.
