## cue 01
The formula editing screen. This is where you apply the update suggestions from the previous chapter to the actual search strategy. Each block gets one line, and you can rewrite any of them directly.

## cue 02
Hovering over a line reveals a pencil icon. Pressing it makes just that line editable. Here we'll add the MeSH term suggested in the previous chapter to block 2.

## cue 03
Add the term and press Save, and it's reflected in the strategy right away. This save is only within the screen — nothing is recorded yet.

## cue 04
Once you've finished your edits, note the reason for the change in the edit memo. Then press "Save as new version," and a new version is recorded with the original as its parent. The previous strategy isn't lost — you can trace it later in the history.

## cue 05
This is the result of re-validating with the term added. The paper that was being missed is now retrieved, and the capture rate has reached 100 percent. Building a search strategy is this loop: validate, fix, validate again.
