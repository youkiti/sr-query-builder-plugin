## cue 01
This is the block approval screen. A block is a cluster of concepts used to build a search strategy. You split what you're looking for into groups — the condition, the treatment, the study design — list synonyms under each, and combine them at the end. That's the basic shape of a PubMed search strategy.

## cue 02
Three blocks were extracted from our protocol. Block 1 is ARDS, block 2 is ECMO, and block 3 is a filter narrowing to randomized controlled trials.

## cue 03
Every block has a name and a description, and both are editable. The description is the important one: it's what the AI uses as a clue when working out synonyms and MeSH terms, so the more concretely it states which papers you want to capture, the better the search terms you'll get.

## cue 04
Below is the combination expression, which specifies how the blocks are joined — here, block 1 AND block 2 AND block 3. Joining everything with AND is usually fine. The syntax is checked as you type.

## cue 05
Once you're happy with it, press approve. The content is recorded, and you move on to registering seed papers.
