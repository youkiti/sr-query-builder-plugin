## cue 01
With the strategy settled, it's time to export. At the top is a link that opens your finished strategy directly in PubMed — press it and PubMed opens already searched with the current strategy.

## cue 02
Systematic reviews search more than PubMed alone. This button rewrites the finished strategy for four other databases, because each one supports different operators and tags — pasting the same text everywhere won't work.

## cue 03
Here are the conversions: CENTRAL, Embase on Dialog, ClinicalTrials.gov, and ICTRP. Open each one to see the converted strategy. If anything was dropped or needs attention, it appears here as a warning. Don't use them blind — read these first.

## cue 04
Each database's strategy can be saved as a Markdown file. Search strategies are often submitted as a paper's appendix, so keeping them in this form is handy.

## cue 05
At the bottom is boilerplate text for your paper's Methods section, in English and Japanese. It states that a generative AI assisted in drafting the search strategy, which model was used, and that the authors reviewed the content. Press copy and adjust it as needed.
