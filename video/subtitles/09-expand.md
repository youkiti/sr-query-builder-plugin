## cue 01
Interactive seed expansion — an experimental feature, as the banner says. Just outside the range your current strategy retrieves, there may be papers you'd actually want to include that simply aren't matching. This searches that zone mechanically and asks you to judge what it finds.

## cue 02
Pressing this button builds a broadened strategy by loosening the current one along two axes, then searches only the margin outside it. It runs through six stages, from proposing broadening terms to searching the margin, removing duplicates, and having the AI select among the candidates.

## cue 03
Three boundary cases were found. The current strategy returns 4 records, broadening it gives 8, and 3 were selected from the margin between them. Each card explains why the case is borderline, and the selected card shows its abstract so you can read it before deciding.

## cue 04
There are three verdicts: include, exclude, and maybe. You can use the buttons, or the keys i, e, and m. After a verdict the focus moves to the next card automatically, so you can work straight through. Papers you include are registered as seed papers.

## cue 05
Once everything is judged, the round completes and re-validation runs immediately. With more seeds, the capture rate drops to 4 out of 6. That isn't a regression; it means a gap has become visible. Below are update suggestions: which terms to add to which block to retrieve those papers.

## cue 06
These suggestions are not applied automatically. In the next chapter, on the formula editing screen, you decide for yourself whether to adopt them.
