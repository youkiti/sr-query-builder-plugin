## cue 01
This video explains how to use SR Query Builder Plugin, a Chrome extension that helps you build search strategies for systematic reviews.

## cue 02
This is the workspace. Once you enter a research protocol, a generative AI drafts a PubMed search strategy, validates that it retrieves your seed papers, and converts it for other databases.

## cue 03
The side navigation lists all ten steps of the workflow: from protocol input through block approval, seed papers, and formula generation and validation, all the way to export. Work through them from top to bottom and you'll end up with a complete search strategy.

## cue 04
Of the three tools for systematic reviews, this extension handles search strategy development. The records you gather here are handed off to the next tool for screening. Let's start with the setup.
