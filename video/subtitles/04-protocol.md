## cue 01
The first step: protocol input. What you enter here becomes the foundation for the block extraction and everything else that follows. There are two ways to provide it — typing or pasting directly into the screen, or uploading a file. This video uses direct input.

## cue 02
Paste the full text of your protocol into the field. There's no need to tidy up the formatting. As long as it contains the research question, the population, intervention, comparison and outcomes, plus the inclusion and exclusion criteria, the AI will read the rest.

## cue 03
Here we'll paste a protocol written in PICO format. The population is adults with acute respiratory distress syndrome, the intervention is extracorporeal membrane oxygenation — ECMO — the comparison is conventional mechanical ventilation, and the outcomes are in-hospital and 28-day mortality. Inclusion criteria are randomized controlled trials and adults aged 18 or over; exclusion criteria are pediatric studies, case reports and reviews.

## cue 04
If you've already written your protocol as a Markdown file, switch the input format to file upload and just select that file to do the same thing. The format is detected automatically from the file extension.

## cue 05
Once it's entered, press this analyze button. The AI reads the protocol and then extracts candidate search blocks. Elapsed time is displayed, so give it a moment.

## cue 06
When extraction finishes, you move automatically to the next screen: block approval. From here on, it's the human's turn to review what the AI proposed.
