## cue 01
Clicking the extension icon in your toolbar opens this project selection screen. The first time you use it, a "Sign in with Google" button appears here. Press it and grant access, and you'll end up in the state shown now, with your signed-in email address displayed.

## cue 02
This extension treats one review as one project. Creating one sets up a Google Sheets spreadsheet and a Drive folder, and the protocol, the search strategies and the validation results are all recorded there — ready to share, and to audit later.

## cue 03
Enter a title and press Create. Our review question here: does ECMO improve survival in adults with ARDS?

## cue 04
The main view opens in a new tab. The first screen is Protocol input. The later items in the side navigation are still grayed out — you can't move ahead until the preceding step is done. From the second time on, just pick the project from "Recent projects" to resume.
