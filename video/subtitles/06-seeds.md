## cue 01
Registering seed papers. A seed paper is one you already know your search strategy absolutely must retrieve — an included study from a previous review, or a paper you've already read and judged eligible. Registering them lets you measure, mechanically, what percentage of them your finished strategy actually captures.

## cue 02
To register, just paste in PMIDs. Separate them with line breaks or commas and enter them in bulk. You can also export a file from your PubMed results and upload that instead.

## cue 03
Pressing Register queries PubMed and pulls in the bibliographic records. Here, all five were registered as valid papers. If a PMID doesn't exist, or is already registered, the breakdown appears here too.

## cue 04
Expanding the details in the list shows the title, authors, abstract, and even the MeSH terms assigned to that paper. Use this to confirm these are the papers you meant to register. Unchecking a row temporarily excludes that paper from validation.

## cue 05
With the seed papers in place, it's time to generate the search strategy.
